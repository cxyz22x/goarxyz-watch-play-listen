Privacy

goarxyz is a front-end catalog. There is no account and no goarxyz login. This page says what the site itself stores.

On this device

  • Saved lists, region, and the position of the menu button, in local storage.
  • Short-lived catalog responses in memory while a tab is open.

Clearing site data for this address removes that. goarxyz does not run its own analytics pixel.

Other services

Posters, titles, and playback can come from other sites, including The Movie Database, AniList, image hosts, and the player you open. Those services see the request your browser makes and have their own policies.
